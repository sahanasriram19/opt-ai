How Pulse Works

Five Phases, One Autonomous Run

One upload triggers a headless AI QA Expert that orchestrates specialised agents to deliver a complete suite of testing assets.

1

Scope

Identifies the in-scope technologies, components and inputs to establish the context for discovery. Output: in-scope inventory, technology stack, assumptions & inputs, test objectives.

2

Discover

Reverse engineers the application to discover system boundaries, dependencies, data flows and business rules. Output: system map, dependencies, business rules, test scenarios.

3

Observe (optional, when runtime logs are provided)

Analyses runtime logs and telemetry to understand actual behaviour and data patterns. Output: behaviour insights, data patterns, usage flows, risk indicators.

4

Generate

AI agents generate test cases, synthetic data and automation scripts aligned to discovered coverage and risks. Output: test cases, synthetic data, automation scripts, test data sets.

5

Assemble

Assembles all assets, links them to requirements and delivers traceability, coverage and audit reports. Output: traceability matrix, coverage mapping, test plan, generation report.

More About Pulse

Headless. Intelligent. Autonomous. Pulse's AI QA Expert orchestrates specialised agents to get the job done — no manual coordination, no context switching.

Overview

Pulse is Optimum's headless, autonomous AI QA Expert. Upload your testing inputs once — requirements, source code, database schemas, APIs, business rules and supporting documents — and Pulse orchestrates specialised agents to analyse your application, discover its tested surface, and generate a comprehensive, connected suite of testing assets, autonomously.

Generated assets include a test strategy and plan, positive/negative/edge test cases, synthetic test data, framework-aligned automation scripts, a traceability matrix, coverage mapping, and audit-ready generation reports.

How It's Built

Pulse's QA Expert AI understands your context, sets the plan, and orchestrates six specialised agents working in parallel under shared memory and context: Discovery, Test Design, Test Data, Automation, Coverage and Reporting.

It is fully headless — no UI to manage — and deploys as cloud or on-prem SaaS, integrating natively with Jira, Confluence, GitLab, SonarQube, Nexus IQ, Nexus Repository, Twistlock, ServiceNow and more.

Security & Compliance

Pulse is secure and governed by design: it does not train on your data, and its Synthetic Data Engine is privacy- and security-aware, with built-in support for masking and sensitive (PII) data handling.

Every generated asset — test cases, scripts, data and reports — remains linked to its source requirement, code and discovered boundary for full traceability and audit readiness.

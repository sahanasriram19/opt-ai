More About Forge

Forge is more than an AI assistant — it's an AI Engineering System purpose-built to run the complete software engineering lifecycle.

Overview

Forge is an enterprise-grade platform that orchestrates specialist AI agents — Analyst, Architect, Developer, Reviewer, Tester, DevOps and Operations — across the software delivery lifecycle, securely, intelligently and at scale.

The Forge Project Manager coordinates every agent, context and workflow end-to-end: from intent, to plan, to execution, to delivery, to continuous learning — ensuring the right work gets done, the right way.

How It's Built

Forge runs on a shared Context Engine and knowledge graph, an Intelligent Workflow Engine, dynamic Agent Handoff, and a Decision Layer for human-in-the-loop approvals. It is model-agnostic (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Azure OpenAI or enterprise models) and deployable on-prem, in a private cloud, or across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

Native connectors reach your existing toolchain — Jira, Confluence, GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps, SonarQube, Nexus and more — with open APIs and SDKs to extend further.

Security & Compliance

Forge Studio brings governance, approvals, auditability and real-time observability across every agent and workflow: policy-driven controls, role-based access, immutable audit trails and evidence packs aligned to frameworks such as MAS, ISO, SOC, OWASP and NIST.

Data isolation, encryption and tenant controls ensure your data and models stay entirely within your control.

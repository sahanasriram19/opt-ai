How It Works

The Agent Lifecycle

Every agent in AI Studio moves through the same five governed stages, from creation to continuous improvement.

1

Build

Create and configure skills, agents and capabilities. Skills are reusable playbooks that define expertise and behaviour; agents are specialised and custom-built with defined skills, tools and guardrails.

2

Govern

Apply organisational policies, guardrails, permissions and approval workflows before agents run — with human-in-the-loop review at the right points in every workflow.

✓ Policy check ✓ Scoped permissions ✓ Approval routed
3

Run

Deploy agents with controls for safe and trusted execution — scoped permissions, fine-grained access to data, tools and capabilities.

4

Observe

Monitor performance, usage, quality and outcomes in real time: execution intelligence, latency and accuracy, token/cost usage, and dashboards & reports for every stakeholder.

5

Improve

Evaluate, learn and continuously optimize agents and skills based on real-world outcomes, quality evaluations and feedback.

More About AI Studio

AI Studio is the unified platform to build, govern, operate and observe agents across the enterprise.

Overview

AI Studio is Optimum's Enterprise AI Control Plane. It rests on four foundations — People & Teams, Data & Knowledge, Tools & Integrations, and Models — and lets you build on top with reusable Skills, specialised Agents, and composable Capabilities that solve business problems and drive outcomes.

AI Studio is the plane that connects Optimum's other AI platforms — Forge, Pulse and CohesionX — into One Enterprise AI Portfolio, unified by governance and assured by design.

How It's Built

Every agent moves through the same Agent Lifecycle: Build (create and configure skills, agents and capabilities), Govern (apply policies, guardrails, permissions and approval workflows), Run (deploy with controls for safe execution), Observe (monitor performance, usage, quality and outcomes), and Improve (evaluate, learn and continuously optimize).

Observability spans Execution Intelligence, Performance & Quality, Usage & Cost, and real-time Dashboards & Reports for every stakeholder.

Security & Compliance

Governance, security and compliance are built in from the start — guardrails, scoped permissions, human-in-the-loop approvals, audit trails and policy enforcement all working together.

  • Safety & Guardrails — prevent misuse and protect the enterprise
  • Scoped Permissions — fine-grained access control
  • Approvals & Workflows — human-in-the-loop review for sensitive actions
  • Audit Trails — immutable logs of every action and decision
  • Policy Compliance & Data Security — enforce enterprise policies and standards

AI Studio is enterprise-ready by design: secure by default, private by design, role-based access, multi-tenant isolation, and compliance ready.

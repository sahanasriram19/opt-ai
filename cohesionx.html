How CohesionX Works

From Execution to Evidence

CohesionX controls AI activity before it runs, understands it at runtime, and proves it after the fact — with a tamper-proof record of every decision.

1

Control Before Execution

Every agent carries an Agent Passport and operates under a Permit-to-Act, so only authorised actions are allowed to run in the first place.

2

Understand & Optimize at Runtime

Prove With Evidence

More About CohesionX

Trust & Governance, Risk & Compliance, Faster Investigation, Continuous Improvement — built for enterprise from the ground up.

Overview

Enterprise AI decisions need to be reconstructable across systems. CohesionX unifies inputs from models, agents, tools and systems into a single AI Decision Record, so any decision — however it was made — can be reconstructed, replayed and proven after the fact.

The result is verifiable evidence and end-to-end assurance for every AI-driven action across your enterprise, packaged into regulator-ready reporting.

How It's Built

CohesionX controls activity before execution with an Agent Passport and Permit-to-Act model, understands and optimises behaviour at runtime by ingesting from sources like Langfuse, LangChain and Splunk, then proves outcomes with evidence stored in a Decision Vault.

Every record is sealed with a Digital Notary into a tamper-proof form, deployable in cloud or on-prem environments and model agnostic by design.

Security & Compliance

CohesionX is enterprise secure — records are tamper-proof, evidence is verifiable, and reporting is built to satisfy regulators, auditors and internal risk & compliance teams alike.

Faster investigation and continuous improvement follow naturally from having a single, trustworthy source of truth for every AI decision made across the enterprise.
